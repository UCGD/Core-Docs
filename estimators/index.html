Before you send a quote

Estimates are planning figures, not invoices. Confirm current rates on the UCGD core facility, CHPC and AWS pricing pages, and check scope with a UCGD analyst before quoting a fixed amount.

Editing rates

Each estimator has a “Pricing assumptions” panel. Changes are saved in your browser only, so other people still see the published defaults. Use “Reset to default rates” to return to them.
