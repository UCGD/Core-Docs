Service

Per-sample rates step down by volume: the first 100 samples are billed at the top rate, samples 101–500 at the next, and so on. A $230 minimum applies.

$230 setup plus $2 per sample. For combining samples from separate projects where variant calling is already done; no variant calling is charged.

Analyst hours

Both billed at $115/hr. Post-VCF covers downstream discovery work with collaborating analysts; custom development covers bespoke software and workflows.

Extra storage & backup

WGS and WES samples already include one year of storage, backup and Mosaic access. Add years beyond that here at $85/TB/year.

Quote adjustments

Leave at 0 for published UCGD rates. External academic and commercial rates are set by UCGD on request.

02Variant calling by volume tier

How the per-sample charge builds up across the volume tiers. Each tier only applies to the samples that fall inside it.

  • Joint genotyping combines samples from separate projects where variant calling has already been done. It is quoted on its own, without variant calling charges.
  • The UCGD rate page lists the included year of storage for WGS, WES and RNA samples only, so it is not assumed here. Use the extra storage fields if the client needs storage.
  • Variant calling includes consult time with the UCGD bioinformatics team, receipt of data from the sequencing vendor, one year of storage and access at CHPC, and final storage after the project ends.
  • With "Both" selected, WGS and WES samples are tiered separately, each on its own rate card. The $230 minimum applies to the combined variant calling charge.
