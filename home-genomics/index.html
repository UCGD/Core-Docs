// the pipeline

From reads to reviewed variants

Every UCGD project follows the same automated path, orchestrated with Nextflow inside the CHPC Protected Environment.

  1. 01InputFASTQ · BAM · CRAMSequencer output or existing alignments
  2. 02Align & QCCRAMfastp, alignstats, peddy, goleft
  3. 03CallgVCFDeepVariant per sample, plus Manta and Smoove SVs
  4. 04Joint genotypeVCFGLnexus across the cohort and background samples
  5. 05AnnotateVCFVEP and Calypso post-processing
  6. 06MosaicProjectResults delivered for review and analysis
